## Tips

- `get_campaign_request_url` is a **write tool** — it creates a real review or survey request against the customer's record, not just a preview of a link.
- Pass `templateId` for a review request or `surveyId` for a survey request, never both. Resolve survey ids with [`get_all_surveys`](/mcp/tools/surveys/get-all-surveys).
- Resolve `customerId` from the user or from a prior [`upsert_contact`](/mcp/tools/contact/upsert-contact) call — never guess it.
