AI Search provides a comprehensive view of your business performance across AI-powered search platforms, including data accuracy, sentiment analysis, citations, brand ranking, and overall visibility.

## Notes

### Filtering by Brand or Location

All AI Search public APIs support two mutually exclusive filtering modes:

- **Brand filter** (`brands`): Pass one or more brand names to aggregate data at the brand level.
- **Location filter** (`businessNumbers`): Pass one or more business numbers to scope data to specific locations.

> **Important:** Providing both `brands` and `businessNumbers` in the same request will return a `500` error. Use one or the other, never both.

### Time-Bucketed Responses

APIs that return time-series data (overtime, competitor, share of voice) group results into date buckets. The bucketing frequency — daily, weekly, or monthly — is determined by the data collection schedule configured for your account at both the brand and location level.

### AI Sites

If `aiSites` is omitted or empty, the response includes all AI platforms enabled for your account (e.g. ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok). Pass specific site names to narrow results to those platforms only.
