title: "AI Search"
---

Get your business's accuracy, sentiment, citations, ranking, and visibility across AI platforms.

### Filter by brand or location

Every AI Search API filters by brand or by location, not both

- **Brand** (`brands`): Pass brand names to get data for each brand.
- **Location** (`businessNumbers`): Pass business numbers to get data for specific locations.

> **Important:** Send `brands` or `businessNumbers`, not both. A request with both returns an error.

### Responses grouped by date

APIs that return data over time, such as over time, competitor, and share of voice, group results by date. Each group covers a day, week, or month, based on the data collection schedule set for your account.

### AI Platforms

If you leave out {aiSites} or send it empty, the response covers every AI platform enabled for your account, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Grok. To narrow results, pass platform names.
